# Escalation Policy

## Escalation Levels

### Level 1 - Team Level
Handled by engagement team. Typical issues:
- Minor scope clarification
- Resource allocation adjustments
- Routine schedule changes (< 1 week)

### Level 2 - Practice Director
Escalate when:
- Budget variance exceeds 10%
- Schedule variance exceeds 2 weeks
- Client satisfaction drops below 6/10
- Critical resource gaps
- SLA breach imminent

### Level 3 - Managing Partner
Escalate when:
- Budget variance exceeds 20%
- Schedule variance exceeds 4 weeks
- Client threatens termination
- Legal/compliance issues
- Reputational risk

### Level 4 - Executive Leadership
Escalate when:
- Strategic account at risk
- Contract value > $5M impacted
- Regulatory investigation
- Media attention

## Escalation Process
1. Document issue and impact assessment
2. Notify next level within 24 hours
3. Schedule alignment call within 48 hours
4. Agree on remediation plan with owners and dates
5. Track progress weekly until resolution
6. Close with lessons learned

## Communication Protocols
- Internal: Slack #escalations channel + email
- Client: Formal written notification within 24 hours of Level 2+
- Documentation: All escalations logged in engagement tracker
