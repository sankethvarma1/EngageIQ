# Margin Management

## Target Margins by Engagement Type
- Strategic Consulting: 45-55%
- Implementation: 35-45%
- Advisory: 50-60%
- Managed Services: 25-35%

## Margin Levers
1. **Utilization**: Target 75-85% billable for consultants
2. **Rate Optimization**: Annual rate reviews, tier-based pricing
3. **Scope Control**: Rigorous change request process
4. **Leverage Model**: Optimal partner/director/manager/consultant/analyst ratio
5. **Offshore/Onshore Mix**: Target 30-40% offshore for implementation

## Margin Monitoring
- Weekly: Engagement manager reviews timesheet actuals vs plan
- Monthly: Practice director reviews P&L by engagement
- Quarterly: Portfolio review with finance

## Warning Signals
- Margin below 20% for 2 consecutive months
- Utilization below 60% for 3 consecutive weeks
- Budget variance > 15% without approved change requests
- Overtime > 15% of billable hours

## Recovery Actions
- Immediate: Scope freeze, resource reallocation
- Short-term: Renegotiate timeline, add offshore resources
- Medium-term: Rate increase discussion, scope reduction
- Structural: Engagement restructuring, team recomposition
